The Process

About Us

We're Changing How Kenyans Buy & Sell Cars.

TrueDrive Kenya was born out of frustration with the way the used car market works — dodgy listings, tampered odometers, time-wasting tyre-kickers, and deals that fall through at the last minute.

We're a professional car brokerage based in Kenya. We don't own cars — we represent sellers and help buyers find trustworthy options. Think of us as your personal car agent, working on commission, so our interests are 100% aligned with yours.

We operate with transparency, proper documentation, and a genuine commitment to making every deal go smoothly for both sides.
